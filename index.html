02 / SMALL DESIGN CHOICES

Predictable by design.
Flexible by nature.

Clear next steps

A visible sequence and simple invitations help a facilitator explain what is happening now and what could happen next.

Quiet by default

No sounds, flashing effects, or automatic activity changes. The timer is optional, and reaching zero never forces the next step.

Privacy stays simple

Plans remain in this browser unless you export them. Use activity descriptions without names or personal records.
